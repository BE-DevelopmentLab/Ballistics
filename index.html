Hit chance

--first-round hit chance
Uses the current solution. Errors are typical (one standard deviation): how far off your wind call or range usually is. A laser rangefinder is about 1 to 2 yd; a guessed range is often 5% or more. Group size is the extreme spread of your usual groups (it covers you and the rifle); velocity SD comes from a chronograph. BC uncertainty is how far the BC you use could be off: about 2% for a trued BC, 5% or more for a box BC. Only this one target is assessed, and its size matters most: a target smaller than your group gives a low chance however good the hold is.

Sectional density = weight (gr) / 7000 / diameter². Saved with every shot and load test. Use in profile copies the BC and model into Rifle and load, plus the weight and diameter when the bullet length and twist are filled in there.

True the load

Uses this profile's shots from 300 yd out (300 to 600 yd is enough). Each shot is solved in the conditions it was fired in, on the phone, so Solstice can be off. With a chronographed velocity only the BC is trued, which gives a much firmer BC.

Group from a photo

Shoot the target square-on and fill the frame. Then tap two points a known distance apart (grid lines or a ruler) to set the scale.
Mark holes: tap each hole (tap again to remove it), or drag a circle to move it. Scale points and the aim point can be dragged too. Find holes looks for holes once the scale is set; holes in a black aiming mark need a tap.
